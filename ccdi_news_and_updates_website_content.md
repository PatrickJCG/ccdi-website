## Smart Climate Controller Systems for Commercial Poultry Houses
*October 2, 2026 | Technology & Equipment*

[CCDI](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.facebook.com%2Fccdi.asia%2Fphotos) now offers cloud-connected climate controller systems designed for real-time poultry house monitoring and remote management via smartphone or computer. Key capabilities include:
* **8 Temperature Sensors:** Captures a broader multi-zone view of house conditions.
* **Complete Environmental Monitoring:** Tracks temperature, humidity, air velocity, and static pressure in one system.
* **Integrated Production Tracking:** Monitors climate data alongside water consumption, feed intake, and flock mortality records.
